About

I am an Associate Professor of Economics at Deakin University. My research lies at the intersection of Macroeconomics and Monetary Economics, Contract Theory, Development, and Finance, with a focus on credit market frictions, financial regulation, and financial innovation.

Research Interests

  • Macroeconomics and Monetary Policy
  • Development Economics
  • Credit Market Frictions
  • Financial Institutions and Services
  • Digital Payments and Financial Innovation
